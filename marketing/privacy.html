Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how tScrub ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal data when you use our website or software. We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

2. Data controller

tScrub is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can contact us at hello@tscrub.com. [Insert registered company name, company number, and registered address when available.]

3. Scope and data minimisation

We collect the minimum personal data necessary to provide the service. We do not collect data beyond what is needed for the purposes below, and we do not engage in profiling.

6. How we share your data

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the service (for example, an email delivery provider), under written contracts that require equivalent protection. We may disclose data where required by law, by a court, or by a regulator such as the ICO or the FCA.

7. International transfers

We store and process personal data within the UK. Where a service provider transfers data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out above, or as required by law. Enquiry correspondence is retained for up to 12 months unless a longer period is required; marketing contact details are retained until you withdraw consent or ask us to delete them.

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. These include access controls, encryption in transit, and secure processing agreements with suppliers. We will notify you and the ICO of a personal data breach where required to do so.

10. Automated decision-making

We do not use personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

11. Children's data

Our service is intended for business users and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where consent is the basis).

To exercise any right, email us at the address above. We will respond within one month (extendable by a further two months for complex requests) and may ask you to verify your identity. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

13. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website is static and does not set tracking cookies or serve advertising. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a request to Google's servers; Google's processing is subject to its own privacy policy.

14. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be notified where required.

16. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, contact us in the first instance. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection.

This is a template policy. Before relying on it for a live business, have it reviewed by a solicitor qualified in English law.
