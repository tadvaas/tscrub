Pricing questions

Do you charge VAT?

VAT is added at the applicable UK rate at checkout. Business customers outside the UK may be zero-rated subject to valid VAT registration details.

What counts as a device?

A device is one physical drive sanitised in a calendar month. Volumes above your plan move to the next tier automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Team is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time. You keep your sanitisation reports even after downgrading.

Build your own appliance

  1. 1. Download the tScrub script and the builder.
  2. 2. Run the builder against a stock Linux live image.
  3. 3. Flash the resulting image to USB (or serve it over PXE).
  4. 4. Boot any machine and tScrub starts automatically.

System requirements

  • • x86-64 machine with USB or PXE boot
  • • Linux kernel with root access
  • • 1 GB RAM (erasure runs in parallel)
  • • Drives must not be in use by the host OS

What's included

  • • The self-contained tScrub script
  • • The appliance builder
  • • Embedded sedutil-cli for SED/OPAL handling
  • • SHA-256 checksums for every release

Verify your download

Always verify the checksum before building your appliance:

425dce8629ebfa38a671d992e884bbfcfbfecfc558113d0241acac734d21c3b5  tscrub.sh

Compare the output against this checksum, also published at /downloads/tscrub.sh.sha256.

Verify the release signature (proves the file came from tScrub):

openssl pkeyutl -verify -pubin -inkey tscrub.pub -rawin -in tscrub.sh -sigfile tscrub.sh.sig
# → Signature Verified Successfully

Grab tscrub.pub and tscrub.sh.sig from /downloads/ alongside the script.

Signed reports verify against the tScrub vendor key — fingerprint be81586c42b5fb2451f7691782c08376c2038d277e79710ff45294409b476c02. See the docs for the full key.
