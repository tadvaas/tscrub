Pricing questions

Do you charge VAT?

VAT is added at the applicable UK rate at checkout. Business customers outside the UK may be zero-rated subject to valid VAT registration details.

What counts as a device?

A device is one physical drive sanitised in a calendar month. Volumes above your plan move to the next tier automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Team is billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time. You keep your sanitisation reports even after downgrading.

Build your own appliance

  1. 1. Issue your licence, then download the tScrub script from your dashboard.
  2. 2. Boot a Linux live environment (ShredOS or your own) from USB or PXE.
  3. 3. Place the .lic alongside tscrub.sh and run it.
  4. 4. tScrub wipes every supported drive and writes the chain-of-custody report.

System requirements

  • • x86-64 machine with USB or PXE boot
  • • Linux kernel with root access
  • • 1 GB RAM (erasure runs in parallel)
  • • Drives must not be in use by the host OS

What's included

  • • The self-contained tScrub script
  • • Embedded sedutil-cli for SED/OPAL handling
  • • Pre/post-wipe SMART capture for every drive
  • • Chain-of-custody report + Certificate of Destruction generation
  • • SHA-256 checksums and Ed25519 release signatures
