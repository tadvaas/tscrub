Get started with tScrub

From account to Certificate of Destruction in eight steps. No vendor hardware, no lock-in — just a script you can read and a trail you can prove.

01

Create your account

Register at tscrub.com/register as a personal or company account, verify your email, then sign in.

02

Get a licence

Every tScrub build needs a licence — even the free tier. Free licences are self-serve from the Pricing page and produce unsigned (checksum-only) reports. Paid licences (pay-as-you-go, Team, Enterprise) add signed reports, a signed certificate, and a QR code — they're issued by the tScrub team, so contact us.

03

Download the script and licence

Sign in and open your dashboard's Licence page to download tscrub.sh (plus its checksum and signature) and your .lic file.

04

Boot the appliance

Boot a Linux live environment (ShredOS or your own) from USB or PXE, place the .lic alongside tscrub.sh, and run it. No installation needed.

05

Run a sanitisation job

tScrub prompts for a 5-digit Chain of Custody ID, discovers every drive and maps it to its strongest NIST 800-88 method, then wipes all supported drives in parallel — capturing pre- and post-wipe SMART data.

06

Get the report out

The report (CSV + signature + manifest) is written to the boot USB automatically, or you can push it straight to your dashboard (tscrub_upload= + tscrub_api_token=, needs curl — not present on stock ShredOS) or FTP (shredos_output=).

07

Generate a Certificate of Destruction

Back in the dashboard, the Certify page turns the report files into a printable Certificate of Destruction per Chain of Custody ID — device annex (Annex A) plus pre/post SMART annex (Annex B). Paid licences get a digitally signed PDF with a QR code.

08

Verify anytime

Anyone can scan the certificate's QR code — or visit tscrub.com/verify?cert=COD-… — to confirm the certificate against the issuance record, including a SHA-256 of the PDF itself.

Command reference

tscrub                          # full run (wipes all supported drives)
tscrub --dry-run                # discover and classify, wipe nothing
tscrub --output /mnt/usb        # write the report to /mnt/usb
tscrub --license /path/x.lic    # supply the licence explicitly
tscrub verify report.csv        # verify a report (SHA-256 + signature)

Full documentation: tscrub.com/docs.
