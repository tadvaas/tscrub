Chain of custody, built in

Each run is tied to a Chain of Custody ID and captures drive model, serial, per-drive method, certification, and final status. The report is the evidence — hand it to your auditor as-is.

What the report proves

  • Which method ran — the exact sanitise operation for each drive (crypto purge, enhanced erase, and so on).
  • What outcome it achieved — PURGE, CLEAR, or flagged for physical destruction.
  • Where and when — drive serials, Chain of Custody ID, and timestamp.
  • What failed — frozen, locked, and blocked drives are recorded, never silently marked clean.
