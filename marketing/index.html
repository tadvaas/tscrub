NVMe sanitise

Crypto, block, and overwrite sanitise, plus format fallback — with live SSTAT progress monitoring.

ATA secure erase

Enhanced and standard security erase for SATA/ATA drives, with frozen-drive handling.

SED / OPAL unlock

PSID revert for locked self-encrypting drives, plus detection of BIOS Block SID lockdown (0x4286).

Chain of custody

COCID, drive serials, and per-drive final status — a verifiable CSV report, plus a Certificate of Destruction you can generate in seconds.

SMART capture

Pre- and post-wipe SMART health — status, temperature, power-on hours, and wear — recorded for every drive.
