All prices are in GBP and exclude VAT. Free licences are self-serve from your dashboard; PAYG credit packs are self-serve from the Billing page; Team and Enterprise plans are set up by the tScrub team — contact us.

Pricing questions

Do you charge VAT?

VAT is added at the applicable UK rate at checkout. Business customers outside the UK may be zero-rated subject to valid VAT registration details.

What counts as a device?

A device is one physical drive sanitised. Pay-as-you-go is £3 per device, sold in prepaid packs of 10 / 50 / 100. Team includes 100 credits and Enterprise 500. Larger volumes are handled on an Enterprise basis — contact us.

Can I cancel anytime?

Volume plans are one-off purchases with no ongoing commitment. You keep your sanitisation reports and unused credits stay on your account.
