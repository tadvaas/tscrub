How it works

  1. 1 Upload your reports. Select one or more .csv files from a job — add the matching .json and .csv.sig sidecars if you have them.
  2. 2 We verify. Each report is hashed (SHA-256) and checked against its manifest; where signed, the Ed25519 signature is verified server-side.
  3. 3 We consolidate. Every drive with the same Chain of Custody ID is merged into one certificate, no matter how many runs produced them.
  4. 4 Download your certificate. A tamper-evident PDF with a unique Certificate ID, a full device annex, and a verification QR code.

How verification works

Every certificate is issued with a unique Certificate ID. Scanning the QR code — or opening tscrub.com/verify?cert=COD-… — confirms it against the issuance record and shows a SHA-256 hash of the document itself, so anyone can prove it hasn't been altered since it was issued.

What you get

  • • One PDF certificate per Chain of Custody ID, with every run consolidated.
  • • SHA-256 and signature verification performed server-side.
  • • Per-drive details: model, serial, type, method, certification, final status, and wiped date.
  • • Pre- and post-wipe SMART health (status, temperature, power-on hours, wear) captured in the certificate when present in the report.
  • • A unique Certificate ID and QR code linking to online verification.
